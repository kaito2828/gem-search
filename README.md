## 🚀 What gem-search Does

Memecoins can move fast, and the story behind a coin often matters as much as the chart. gem-search gives you a clearer view of what is happening behind the scenes. It checks for bundles of wallets that may be buying together, finds linked wallets that may belong to the same person or group, and reviews dev history so you can see whether the creator has a pattern.

The app is built around three main parts:

- A scanner for checking memecoins.
- A Telegram bot for quick checks from your phone.
- A live Bundle Index for seeing bundle activity as it happens.

Together, these tools help you turn a quick memecoin glance into a more informed review. You can see whether wallets look connected, whether a launch has unusual bundle activity, and whether the developer has a history worth reviewing.
